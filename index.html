2025 Victorian Guide

The one document that can save (or cost) you $40,000+ before you sign.

Home to Resources to Section 32

1. One-Sentence Answer

A Section 32 is the legally required disclosure pack that forces every Victorian seller to reveal 27 hidden property truths — before the buyer is locked in.

2. Why It Exists

Before 1982 buyers had zero protection. Section 32 flipped the rule: disclose everything or the contract can be torn up.

3. Who Prepares It?

Licensed conveyancer or solicitor only. Vendor signs under penalty of $40,469 fine.

4. Timing Rule

Buyer MUST get it before signing the contract. No exceptions.
